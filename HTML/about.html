About the Project

Empowering college students with knowledge to protect and preserve the environment.

Project Overview

The Environmental Awareness Portal is a web-based mini project designed to educate college students about critical environmental issues and sustainable living practices. The portal provides easy-to-understand information and encourages responsible behavior towards nature and the environment.

Objective

To spread awareness about environmental challenges and motivate students to adopt eco-friendly habits in their daily lives.

Vision

To build a generation of environmentally responsible students who actively contribute to a sustainable and greener future.

Technology

The project is developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap to ensure responsiveness and clean design.

Why This Project Matters

Environmental problems such as pollution, climate change, and deforestation directly affect human health and future generations. By creating this portal, we aim to educate students and inspire small actions that can lead to big changes.
